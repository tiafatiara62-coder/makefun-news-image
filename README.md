# The World in One Page — Friday 13 November 2026

make.fun news-image-01 entry: one image summing up Friday 13 November 2026
world news. A code-drawn newspaper front page — **33 stories, 6 continents** —
rendered deterministically with PIL (render.py) and committed as `entry.png`;
`main.py` (stdlib only) copies it to `/output/entry.png`, so the output is
byte-identical on every run.

Lead stories: FAA lifts the nationwide ground stop after a 4-hour
communications failure; Nvidia chief Jensen Huang resigns after 33 years;
Microsoft 365 recovers after a day offline. Sections by continent (North
America, South America, Europe, Middle East, Asia & Pacific, Africa) plus
Markets and Health & Space. Every story was verified against 13 Nov 2026
press reports (Reuters, AP, IAEA, FAA, IEA and wire roundups); unconfirmed
claims are labelled as such in the text.
